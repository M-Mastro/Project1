# Objective and Motivation:
 The data set chosen was 2025 Disability Compensation Recipient by
Country. We chose this dataset as we felt it would be good analysis to examine countries and their veteran
program recipients. The dataset contains the FIPS code, country name, state name, total disability compensation recipients, SCD (service-connected-disability) ratings from 0-20%, 30-40%, 50-60%, 70-90% and 100%. The dataset also contains the ages of recipients from 17-44, 45-64, 65 plus years and gender male or female. The dataset was collected by the national Center for Veterans Analysis and Statistics, by estimating results from looking at different sources. These sources include Department of Veterans Affairs, Office of Information and Technology, USVETS25 data, Veterans Benefits Administration VETSNET FY 2025 compensation data, and Melissa Data (September 2025) geography data. This data was collected to create veteran statistics and analysis. The information that will be useful will be recipient quantities, ages, gender, states, and countries as they will allow us to perform demographic results compared to the number of disability recipients.

Question 1: Does TX or FL have the highest number of recorded female disability recipients?
This question is interesting as we are examining veteran disabilities outside the college scope, analysing
this base on gender will provide us with insight on receiving disability compensation if affected by gender.As there are less females in the military it will be interesting to compare the ratio of compensated females to female participants with the male ratio. 

Question 2: Does TX or FL have the highest number of recorded male disability recipients?
This question is interesting as we are examining veteran disabilities outside the college scope, analysing
this based on gender will provide us with insight on receiving disability compensation if affected by gender. As there are more males in the military it will be interesting to compare the ratio of compensated males to male participants with the female ratio. 

Question 3: Do ages 45-64 or 65+ have the highest number of recipients across all countries?
This question is interesting as we are going to compare demographics of compensated veterans to better
understand veteran programs outside of the college scope. By receiving these demographics we are
going to be able to continue through more research to understand why certain age groups have higher
numbers of compensated individuals than others. 

# Implementation Details:
To answer Question 1, we will use the state and female recipient columns to calculate the total number of
female disability compensation recipients in each state. We will then compare the totals using a bar graph
to identify the state with the highest number of female recipients. We expect to observe differences
between states, with some having substantially higher recipient counts than others.
